OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease (the "Lease") is made as of August 12, 2026 (the "Lease
Date"), between Beacon Street Property LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability
company ("Landlord"), and Corvara Legal Technologies, Inc., a Delaware
corporation ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 400 (the "Premises") at 88
Beacon Street, Boston, Massachusetts, comprising approximately 12,400
rentable square feet ("RSF"), together with the non-exclusive right to use
common areas of the building.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease is eighty-four (84) months (the "Term"),
commencing October 1, 2026 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring September
30, 2033 (the "Expiration Date"), unless earlier terminated or extended as
provided herein.

3. BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay base rent of $6,850.00 per month for the first
twelve (12) months of the Term, payable in advance on the first day of each
month, with a three percent (3%) increase on each anniversary of the
Commencement Date (the "Escalation").

4. OPERATING EXPENSES. In addition to base rent, Tenant shall pay Tenant's
proportionate share (9.8%) of increases in building operating expenses over
the 2026 base year, estimated monthly and trued up annually within ninety
(90) days after year end.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. On execution, Tenant shall deposit $40,000.00 as security
for the full and faithful performance of Tenant's obligations.

6. USE. The Premises shall be used for general office purposes and for no
other purpose.

7. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Provided Tenant performs its obligations, Tenant shall
peacefully hold and enjoy the Premises during the Term.

8. ALTERATIONS; IMPROVEMENTS. Landlord shall provide a tenant improvement
allowance of $85,000.00 for the build-out of the Premises, disbursed within
thirty (30) days after completion of the work and delivery of lien waivers.
Any alterations require Landlord's prior written consent, except for
non-structural changes costing less than $10,000 in the aggregate.

9. REPAIR. Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good order;
Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, and building systems.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet
all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent,
which may be withheld in Landlord's reasonable discretion, except that Tenant
may assign to a successor in interest by merger or sale of substantially all
of Tenant's assets.

11. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming Landlord as
additional insured.

12. HOLDOVER. If Tenant remains in possession after expiration of the Term
without a new lease, Tenant shall pay base rent at one hundred fifty percent
(150%) of the then-applicable base rent, on a month-to-month basis.

13. SURRENDER. On the Expiration Date, Tenant shall surrender the Premises
in the condition required by this Lease, ordinary wear and tear and casualty
excepted.

14. RENEWAL OPTION. Provided no event of default exists, Tenant shall have
one (1) option to renew the Term for sixty (60) months at then-fair market
rent, exercisable by written notice delivered to Landlord no earlier than
fifteen (15) months and no later than nine (9) months prior to the
Expiration Date.

15. SUBORDINATION. This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage on the
Property. Within sixty (60) days after Tenant's written request, Landlord
shall obtain from the current mortgagee, or use commercially reasonable
efforts to obtain from any future mortgagee, a subordination,
non-disturbance, and attornment agreement ("SNDA") in commercially
reasonable form.

16. BROKER. Landlord and Tenant each represent that they dealt with no
broker in connection with this Lease other than Fenway Commercial Advisors,
and Landlord shall pay Fenway Commercial Advisors' commission pursuant to a
separate agreement.

17. DEFAULT; REMEDIES. Landlord may exercise any remedy available at law or
in equity on an event of default remaining uncured for ten (10) days after
written notice.

18. NOTICES. Notices shall be in writing and delivered by certified mail,
reputable overnight courier, or email with confirmation of receipt, to the
addresses set out in the Basic Lease Information.

19. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of
Massachusetts.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the Lease Date.
