BLUEHARBOR LOGISTICS, INC.
MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
March 12, 2026

A special meeting of the Board of Directors of BlueHarbor Logistics, Inc. (the
"Company") was held at 9:00 a.m. on March 12, 2026, at the Company's
headquarters, 2200 Harbor Way, Suite 500, Oakland, California, and by video
conference.

Directors present: M. Okafor (Chair), L. Whitfield, S. Ramirez, D. Chen.
Directors absent: none. All directors being present, a quorum was established.

1. Acquisition of Ferris Cartage, LLC. The Chair presented the recommendation
   of management to acquire 100% of the membership interests of Ferris Cartage,
   LLC, a Nevada limited liability company providing last-mile cartage services
   in the Central Valley, for a purchase price of $4.2 million in cash, subject
   to a working capital adjustment of $250,000 in either direction.

   Upon motion duly made and seconded, the Board RESOLVED to approve the
   acquisition on the terms presented and to authorize the CEO to execute the
   purchase agreement and related documents.

2. Financing. The CFO reported that the acquisition will be funded from the
   Company's existing revolving credit facility. No new indebtedness is
   required.

3. Adjournment. There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
   9:40 a.m.
