LEASE ABSTRACT — HEADQUARTERS
Property: 2200 Harbor Way, Suite 500, Oakland, California
Prepared by Landlord's counsel; unexecuted abstract for reference only

PARTIES
   Landlord: Harbor Point Realty Trust, L.P.
   Tenant:   BlueHarbor Logistics, Inc.
   Lease date: April 1, 2025 (original); First Amendment: June 15, 2025

PREMISES
   Suite 500, comprising approximately 18,200 rentable square feet, including
   40 reserved parking stalls (unassigned).

TERM
   Sixty (60) months, commencing April 1, 2025, expiring March 31, 2030.

RENT
   Base rent (months 1-12): $42,000.00 per month
   Escalation: 3% annually on each anniversary of the commencement date
   Additional rent: Tenant's proportionate share (11.4%) of operating expenses
   over the 2025 base year.

RENEWAL OPTION
   One (1) option to renew for sixty (60) months at then-fair-market rent,
   exercisable by written notice delivered no earlier than fifteen (15) months
   and no later than nine (9) months prior to lease expiration.

SECURITY DEPOSIT
   Cash security deposit of $100,000, reducible to $50,000 after month 30
   provided no monetary default has occurred.

PARKING
   40 stalls at $95 per stall per month (rates escalate with base rent).

NOTABLE LEASE PROVISIONS
   - Assignment: Landlord consent required; consent deemed given for a sale of
     all or substantially all of Tenant's assets to a successor with a net
     worth at least equal to Tenant's.
   - Holdover: 150% of last-month base rent.
   - Restoration: Premises delivered in "as-is" condition at commencement;
     surrender in broom-clean condition, ordinary wear excepted.
   - SNDA: Landlord shall deliver a subordination, non-disturbance, and
     attornment agreement in form attached as Exhibit F within sixty (60) days
     of Tenant's written request.

BROKER
   Cornish & Voyles (Tenant's broker); Landlord to pay broker fees per
   separate agreement.
