EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT — CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of February 2, 2026
(the "Start Date"), between BlueHarbor Logistics, Inc., a Delaware corporation
(the "Company"), and Priya Nair ("Executive").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES. Executive shall serve as Chief Financial Officer,
reporting to the Chief Executive Officer, with responsibilities customary for
a public-company CFO, including oversight of accounting, treasury, tax, and
investor relations.

2. BASE SALARY. Executive's annual base salary shall be $285,000, payable in
accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices, subject to annual
review by the Compensation Committee.

3. ANNUAL BONUS. Executive is eligible for an annual performance bonus with a
target of 20% of base salary, determined by the Compensation Committee against
board-approved metrics.

4. EQUITY. Executive shall receive a grant of 12,000 stock options under the
2020 Equity Incentive Plan, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff,
subject to the terms of the applicable award agreement.

5. SEVERANCE. If the Company terminates Executive without cause (other than
death or disability) outside the twelve months following a change in control,
Executive shall receive nine (9) months of base salary continuation and six
(6) months of COBRA reimbursement.

6. CHANGE IN CONTROL. In the twelve months following a change in control,
a termination without cause entitles Executive to twelve (12) months of base
salary continuation, a pro-rated target bonus, and full acceleration of
then-unvested options.

7. NON-COMPETE; NON-SOLICITATION. For eighteen (18) months following
termination of employment, Executive shall not (a) provide logistics
management services to a Competitor in any state in which the Company operates,
or (b) solicit any Company employee.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY. Executive shall maintain the confidentiality of Company
proprietary information during and after employment.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
California.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date
first written above.
