MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
Draft v2 — for discussion

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
[●], 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Northwind Robotics, Inc., a
Delaware corporation ("Northwind"), and Vantage Analytics Ltd., a company
organized under the laws of England and Wales ("Vantage"). Northwind and
Vantage are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

RECITALS

The Parties wish to explore a potential partnership relating to warehouse
logistics robotics (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party
may disclose certain confidential information to the other.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by
or on behalf of a Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"),
whether orally, visually, or in tangible form, that is designated as
confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given
the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

1.2 Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(b) was known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or
reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is
Disclosed by a third party without breach of any obligation of confidence.

1.3 Residuals. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, the Receiving Party
shall have no obligation with respect to Confidential Information retained in
the unaided memory of its employees, contractors, or advisors ("Residuals"),
and Residuals shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

2. OBLIGATIONS

2.1 The Receiving Party shall (a) use the same degree of care it uses for its
own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care; (b) not
disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees,
contractors, and advisors who need to know for the Purpose and who are bound
by obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement; and (c) use
Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

3. TERM

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for ten (10)
years, after which the obligations herein survive until the Confidential
Information enters the public domain through no fault of the Receiving Party.

4. NO LICENSE

No license or other right under any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade
secret is granted by this Agreement, whether expressly or by implication.

5. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to
disclose Confidential Information, it shall (where legally permitted) give
prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party and cooperate, at the
Disclosing Party's expense, in seeking a protective order.

6. EQUITABLE RELIEF

6.1 The Parties agree that a breach of this Agreement by Vantage would cause
Northwind irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate.
Accordingly, Northwind shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other
equitable relief without proof of actual damages or the inadequacy of
monetary remedies.

6.2 Northwind shall not be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable
relief for a breach of this Agreement by Vantage unless it first demonstrates
irreparable harm and the inadequacy of monetary damages, and posts a bond in
an amount set by the court.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

The Receiving Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Disclosing
Party from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and
expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to
any breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party, including lost profits
and other consequential damages, without limitation as to amount.

8. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall, within
thirty (30) days, return or destroy (at the Disclosing Party's election) all
Confidential Information in its possession or control and certify such return
or destruction in writing; provided that the Receiving Party may retain one
archival copy for legal compliance purposes.

9. NON-SOLICITATION

For three (3) years following the Effective Date, neither Party shall
directly or indirectly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party
who was involved in the Purpose, without the other Party's prior written
consent.

10. ASSIGNMENT

Vantage may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any affiliate or
to any successor in connection with a merger, sale of all or substantially
all of its assets, or similar transaction, without the consent of Northwind.
Northwind may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of
Vantage, which may be withheld in Vantage's sole discretion.

11. GOVERNING LAW; DISPUTES

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without
regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of the courts of Singapore for any dispute arising out of or
relating to this Agreement.

12. MISCELLANEOUS

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with
respect to its subject matter. Amendments must be in writing and signed by
both Parties. Neither Party may waive any provision except in writing. If any
provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
