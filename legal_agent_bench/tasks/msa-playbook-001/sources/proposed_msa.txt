MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT
Proposed by Draco Cloud Services, Inc. — Draft 3, August 2026

This Master Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by and
between Draco Cloud Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Draco"), and
Kestrel Systems, Inc. ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION; TERM. Draco grants Customer a subscription to the hosted
logistics software (the "Service") for an initial term of thirty-six (36)
months (the "Initial Term"). This Agreement renews automatically for
successive thirty-six (36) month terms unless either party gives written
notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the
then-current term.

2. FEES; PAYMENT. Customer shall pay the fees in the applicable Order Form.
   Invoices are due ninety (90) days after receipt (net-90). Fees for any
   renewal term may be increased by Draco, effective upon renewal, by the
   greater of four percent (4%) plus the annual change in the Consumer Price
   Index (CPI-U) or ten percent (10%).

3. ACCEPTABLE USE. Customer shall use the Service in accordance with the
   Acceptable Use Policy. Draco may suspend the Service for violations after
   written notice and a five (5) business day cure period.

4. CUSTOMER DATA. Customer retains ownership of Customer Data. Draco may
   use aggregated, de-identified usage data to improve the Service.

5. SECURITY; BREACH NOTIFICATION. Draco maintains administrative, technical,
   and physical safeguards consistent with industry standards. Draco shall
   notify Customer of any confirmed security incident affecting Customer Data
   within ten (10) business days of confirmation.

6. IP INDEMNIFICATION. Draco shall defend and indemnify Customer against
   third-party claims that the Service, as provided by Draco and used within
   the scope of this Agreement, infringes such third party's intellectual
   property rights. Draco's obligations under this Section 6 are subject to
   the limitation of liability in Section 8.

7. DATA-BREACH REMEDIES. If Customer Data is lost or corrupted due to Draco's
   negligence, Draco's sole obligation is to use commercially reasonable
   efforts to restore the data from backup.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. EXCEPT FOR CUSTOMER'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS,
   NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE
   BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM (THE "CAP").
   THE CAP APPLIES TO ALL CLAIMS, INCLUDING SECTION 6 INDEMNIFICATION AND
   SECTION 7 DATA OBLIGATIONS. NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT,
   INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

9. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate for material breach uncured
   thirty (30) days after written notice. Otherwise, this Agreement may be
   terminated only by mutual written agreement.

10. GOVERNING LAW; FORUM. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the
    State of Delaware. The exclusive forum for any dispute is the state and
    federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

11. INSURANCE. Draco maintains commercial general liability insurance of
    $2,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of $5,000,000
    per claim.

12. SUPPORT. Draco targets 99.5% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below
    99.5%, Customer receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee,
    capped at 15% of the monthly fee per quarter. Service credits are
    Customer's exclusive remedy for availability failures.

13. GENERAL. This Agreement is the entire agreement of the parties regarding
    its subject matter. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both
    parties. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's
    written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of the
    business.
